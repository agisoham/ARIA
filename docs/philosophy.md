# Philosophy

## What ARIA is
A solo-built algorithmic trading system whose primary product is capability: deep, hands-on
learning in machine learning, quantitative finance, and systems engineering. Building toward a
genuine, regulation-compliant earning engine is a real goal too — but protecting capital is
non-negotiable.

## Core mindset
- From **"predict the market"** to **"manage uncertainty and survive."**
- **Debate before you build** — every major choice is pressure-tested by the council first.
- **Two-layer screening** — no verdict is trusted on one pass (see [methodology](methodology.md)).
- **Prove before you risk** — capital is only deployed after the thing that protects it is proven.

## Goal hierarchy
1. **Learn** — the primary, guaranteed return.
2. **Never lose money** — survivability drives every design decision.
3. **Earn — a real objective**, strictly within all applicable market regulations and broker terms.

The **Dual Barbell** reconciles goals 2 and 3: a conservative Core (~70%) guarantees survival,
while a capped Aggressive Alpha sleeve (~30%) pursues asymmetric upside and is allowed to
fail without ever threatening the Core. The two are kept strictly separate. Ambition expresses
itself as a smarter Alpha sleeve — never a looser Core.

## Guiding principle
The goal is not to get rich quickly through reckless AI trading. It is to engineer a survivable,
well-understood system — learning deeply at every step — while preserving controlled exposure to
rare, asymmetric upside, in every market it touches and always within the rules. Debate before you
build. Prove before you risk. Protect the Core, always.
